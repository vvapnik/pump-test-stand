pump-test-stand · ATmega328

Wiring diagram

Full stand schematic: ATmega328 MCU (DIP-28) with all supporting parts, ACS712-20A with an RC filter, pump MOSFET switch with a flyback diode, LCD1602 over I2C, encoder and start/stop button. The crystal is drawn in grey dashes - it is only needed for the atmega328_external_xtal environment; on a breadboard with atmega328_internal_rc you can leave it out.

Common ground. The 5V circuit (MCU, LCD, encoder) and the 12V circuit (pump) must share one common GND - otherwise ACS712 measurements will be random and the MOSFET may not fully turn on.
AIR/WATER/pressure thresholds in pump_state.h are starting values; calibrate them on this stand (procedure in the project README).
